## What was built

- **KnowledgeBase files may be `.pdf` and `.docx`,** read inside the
  binary. Load and validation of the 9 documents took 0.02 s in every
  format.
- **PDF** (`github.com/ledongthuc/pdf`, BSD-3, from `rsc.io/pdf`, no
  dependencies): rows in reading order. The library gives no font sizes
  for these files, so the structure comes from the layout:
  - a gap wider than 1.4 times the page's usual line spacing ends a
    paragraph;
  - a short line alone, without final punctuation, is a heading.
- **DOCX** (standard library):
  - heading styles become headings (`Heading1`, and `Ttulo1` from Word
    in Portuguese);
  - table rows become "a | b".
- **Admission:**
  - `UNREADABLE_FILE` for a file that does not open;
  - `NO_TEXT` for an image-only (scanned) PDF, since OCR is not done.
- **Tests:**
  - a real PDF (its headings and sections);
  - an image-only PDF and a corrupt file;
  - a DOCX with headings and a table;
  - 7 mutations caught.
  - The first DOCX test passed for the wrong reason: the expected
    output also lacked the Portuguese heading. It was caught while
    writing the test, and both the regex and the expectation were
    fixed.

## Stress PDFs (descriptive, 1 run)

**What the extraction gives:**
- **The price list as a table:** the text is readable, but cells run
  together: "Limpeza (profilaxia)R$ 180".
- **The location page in two columns:** the lines of both columns are
  interleaved: "é o Parque Seguro, na Rua das Como chegar Acácias,
  300…".

**Outcome:** with these two PDFs in place of the originals, the 3 price
and 3 location questions were answered correctly 5 times out of 6. The
model recovered the facts from the garbled text. The sixth ("é de
graça?") misses in retrieval in every format.

Two-column layouts are still a risk. A fact split across the column
boundary could be misread. Reading by column (x positions) is the fix if
real documents need it.

## Limits

- The documents were printed from HTML by Chrome, which gives clean
  text. Real PDFs (scans, exports from design tools, forms) vary more.
- No OCR: a scanned PDF is refused by admission, not read.
- Headings in PDFs are guessed from layout. A heading written as a
  sentence ("Como chegar à clínica.") becomes body text.
