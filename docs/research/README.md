# Research

Each report is a dated record of one measurement: its pre-registration
(what was expected, before the data), the result, and the decision it led
to. They are kept as they were written.

Some reports cite examples, datasets and raw results (`bench/results/`) that
are not part of this repository: the published repository keeps the
traveler (`examples/traveler`) as its only example, and the raw data of the
studies is not published. The traveler's studies are `agency-*`,
`team-*`, `router-*` and `traveler-*`.
